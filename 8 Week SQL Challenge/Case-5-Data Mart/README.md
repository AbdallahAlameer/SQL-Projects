# 🛒 Data Mart: Sustainable Packaging Impact Analysis (SQL Case Study)

## 📝 Introduction
**Data Mart** is a rapidly growing international supermarket that specializes in fresh produce and high-quality grocery items.
In June 2020, the executive management team made a strategic decision to transition all products to sustainable, environmentally friendly packaging. 

The primary objective of this project is to perform a rigorous **Before & After Impact Analysis** to evaluate how this packaging change affected sales performance.
Management needs actionable insights to understand whether the initiative caused a temporary friction or a long-term decline, and to identify which regions and customer segments were most impacted.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
To execute this analysis and ensure data accuracy, the following tools and advanced SQL techniques were utilized:
* **Database / SQL Engine:** PostgreSQL 
* **Advanced SQL Concepts:** 
  * Common Table Expressions (CTEs)
  * Window Functions (for dynamic percentage calculations)
  * Conditional Aggregation (`SUM(CASE WHEN...)`)
  * Date/Time Extraction and Manipulation
  * Defensive SQL (`NULLIF` to prevent division-by-zero errors)
  * Drill-Down Analysis (Multi-dimensional `GROUP BY`)

## 🎯 Case Study Sections & Insights

### 1. Data Cleansing
Before diving into the analysis, a `clean_weekly_sales` table was engineered to standardize the dataset. Key transformations included:
* Extracting specific `week`, `month`, and `year` integers from date strings.
* Handling missing values and categorizing `null` segments into an 'unknown' bucket.
* Mapping complex segment codes into readable `age_band` (e.g., Young Adults, Retirees) and `demographic` (e.g., Couples, Families) categories.
* Engineering an absolute `avg_transaction` metric[cite: 6].

### 2. Data Exploration
Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) uncovered several critical operational baselines:
* **Data Collection Gap:** Over $16 Billion in Retail sales were generated by 'unknown' demographics. This massive volume of unclassified transactions represents a significant missed opportunity for Data Mart to track loyalty and customer behavior.
* **Top Customer Profiles:** When isolating known data, 'Retirees' (specifically 'Families') act as the primary drivers of Retail sales, contributing ~$6.6B.
* **Statistical Integrity:** Identified and avoided the "Average of Averages" mathematical fallacy when calculating transaction sizes, opting instead for absolute division (`SUM(sales) / SUM(transactions)`) to ensure accurate KPI reporting.

### 3. Before & After Analysis (Impact Evaluation)
The core of the study evaluated the financial impact of the sustainable packaging introduced in week 25 of 2020.

* **Short-Term Impact (4-Week Window):** Total sales immediately decreased by **$26.88M (-1.15%)** following the rollout.
*  This initial friction suggested customer hesitation due to altered aesthetics or temporary supply chain bottlenecks.
*  
* **Long-Term Impact (12-Week Window):** Over a broader 12-week period, the decline compounded, reaching a massive **-$152.3M (-2.14%)**.
*  The trend accelerated negatively over time rather than recovering, indicating a lasting rejection or ongoing operational failure .
*  
* **Ruling out Seasonality:** To ensure the drop wasn't a standard summer dip, 2020 was compared against historical data.
*  In 2018, sales *grew* by +1.63%, and in 2019, they only dipped slightly by -0.30%. This confirmed the steep -2.14% drop in 2020 was directly tied to the packaging change, not annual seasonality .

## 💡 Key Business Takeaways & Recommendations
1. **Compounding Losses:** The sustainable packaging initiative proved financially detrimental in 2020. The lack of short-term recovery highlights the need for immediate executive intervention.
2. **Segment Vulnerabilities (Bonus Insight):** A drill-down analysis revealed that **SOUTH AMERICA** and **EUROPE**, particularly on the **Shopify** platform, suffered catastrophic sales drops (ranging from -27% to -42%). Management must prioritize investigating localized supply chain logistics and customer feedback in these specific regions.
3. **Data Infrastructure Enhancement:** Data Mart must aggressively incentivize its loyalty program or redesign the POS data capture process to reduce the dominance of 'unknown' customer profiles in the Retail sector.
